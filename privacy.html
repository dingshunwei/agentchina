1. Who we are

China Agent is a B2B product catalog app operated by Shanghai Zhansui Trading Co., Ltd. (上海湛穗贸易有限公司), registered in Jinshan District, Shanghai, China. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it, and your choices.

Contact: zhansuit@qq.com

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account. We do not collect your name, phone number or email unless you choose to send an inquiry.

2.1 Automatic

  • Anonymous device ID — a random identifier generated on your device. It links your favorites, blocked list and app-open records to this device only; it cannot identify you personally.
  • Usage statistics — app open time, product views and search keywords, used to understand which products are useful.
  • Device type & country — coarse information (e.g. Android, country) used for aggregate statistics.

2.2 Provided by you

  • Inquiry details — when you use “Contact Trader”, we send the name, email, WhatsApp number and message you enter to the trader so they can reply.
  • Favorites and blocked list — content you save or hide on this device.

2.3 Permissions

  • Internet — required to load the catalog and submit inquiries.
  • Microphone — only used when you actively record an audio message; we never record in the background.

3. How we use it

  • Show the product catalog and its media.
  • Deliver your inquiries to the trader you contacted.
  • Remember your favorites and blocked items on this device.
  • Produce aggregate statistics (open counts, popular products, popular keywords).

4. Storage & third parties

  • Data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL, hosted cloud) and media files are served from Cloudflare (R2 CDN). All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Catalog requests and inquiries may pass through our Cloudflare Worker proxy (api.agentchina.uk), which adds rate limiting and hides backend credentials.
  • We do not run advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not share it except when required by law.

5. Retention & your rights

  • There is no account. Most data is linked to your anonymous device ID and is kept only as long as needed to provide the service.
  • You can remove your own favorites and inquiries in the app by clearing app data or uninstalling it.
  • To request deletion of data associated with your device, email zhansuit@qq.com; we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, row-level access control on the database, and server-side rate limiting on public endpoints. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be published on this page with a new effective date.

一、我们收集哪些信息

本 App 无需注册账号。除你主动发送询盘外，我们不收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱。

  • 匿名设备标识：本机随机生成的 ID，用于把你的收藏、屏蔽、打开记录关联到这台设备，无法反向识别你的真实身份。
  • 使用统计：App 打开时间、浏览过的产品、搜索关键词（仅用于统计）。
  • 设备类型与所在国家：仅用于汇总统计（如 Android、国家）。
  • 你提交的询盘：使用「Contact Trader」时，你填写的姓名、邮箱、WhatsApp、留言会发送给对应的贸易商以便回复。
  • 收藏 / 屏蔽记录：你在本机保存或隐藏的内容。

二、权限说明

  • 网络：加载产品目录、提交询盘所必需。
  • 麦克风：仅在你主动录制语音留言时使用，绝不会在后台录音。

三、数据存储与第三方

  • 数据存储于 Supabase（云端 PostgreSQL），媒体文件通过 Cloudflare R2 分发，全程 HTTPS 加密。
  • 目录请求与询盘会经过我们的 Cloudflare Worker 代理（api.agentchina.uk），用于限流与隐藏后端密钥。
  • 我们不投放广告、不出售你的数据；仅在法律要求时披露。

四、数据保留与你的权利

  • 没有账号体系，数据主要关联匿名设备标识，仅在有需要时保留。
  • 你可以清除 App 数据或卸载来移除本机记录。
  • 如需删除与你设备相关的数据，请邮件联系 zhansuit@qq.com，我们将在 30 天内处理。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 面向商务用户，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童信息。

六、安全与政策更新

我们采用 HTTPS 传输、数据库行级访问控制、服务端限流等措施保护数据。政策如有重要更新，将在本页公布并更新生效日期。
