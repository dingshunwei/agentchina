1. Overview

China Agent is a B2B product catalog app. You can browse products and send sourcing inquiries without creating an account. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

2. What we collect

  • Anonymous device ID — a random identifier created on your device, used to remember your favorites and count visits. It cannot identify you.
  • Inquiry details — if you contact a trader, the name, email, WhatsApp number and message you enter are sent so they can reply.
  • Basic usage data — app opens, products viewed and search keywords, used only in aggregate.
  • Microphone — only if you actively record an audio message; never in the background.

3. How we use it

  • To show the catalog and remember your favorites.
  • To deliver your inquiry to the trader you contacted.
  • To understand which products are useful (aggregate statistics).

4. Storage & sharing

  • Data is kept on secure, professionally managed cloud infrastructure and transmitted over encrypted connections.
  • Your inquiry is shared only with the trader you chose to contact.
  • We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we disclose it only if the law requires.

5. Retention & your rights

  • There is no account; data is linked to your anonymous device ID and kept only as long as needed.
  • You can clear your data by clearing the app or uninstalling it.
  • To have data linked to your device deleted, email zhansuit@qq.com — we respond within 30 days.

6. Children & security

The app is for business users, not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information.

7. Changes & contact

We may update this policy; changes are published here with a new effective date.

Contact: zhansuit@qq.com
